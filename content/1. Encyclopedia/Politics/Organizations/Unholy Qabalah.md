# Unholy Qabalah

The Unholy Qabalah is, by all accounts, the most elusive gang in Aldiin. Operating deep within the shadows of Alinton’s underworld, it remains an enigma even to the ITPA, which has only managed to uncover fragmented details about its operations and structure. The gang appears to recruit the most psychotic and unhinged individuals while simultaneously maintaining connections with larger local governmental systems. Its branches seem to appear spontaneously across the city, each isolated from the others with no apparent links tying them together.

However, the true danger of the Unholy Qabalah lies in the misconception of its nature: it is perceived as a functional gang, but in reality, it doesn’t truly exist. The supposed headquarters, The Tomb of the Unknowns, is merely the primary residence of its enigmatic leader, around whom the entire organization revolves. The Qabalah exists only as long as the shadow of terror cast by its leader remains. His influence causes henchmen to align with him and associates to emerge, either driven by his schizophrenic worldview, desperate to secure some semblance of safety and wealth, or staying submissive in hopes of avoiding his volatile whims.

The 'gang' itself is a memetic construct. Newcomers, drawn by the fear the leader instills, adopt the group's name, flaunting its colors and symbols as a mark of ultimate street cred. This façade of power, fueled by fear and the image of danger, satisfies the leader's aims. After all, what could be more nourishing to the spirit than providing a platform for others to indulge their darkest impulses?
