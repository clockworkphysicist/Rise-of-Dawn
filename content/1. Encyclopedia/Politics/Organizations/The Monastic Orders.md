## Structure

The reason for why different monastic orders have different rituals, vows, and responsibilities in Aldiin is based on a complex history.

Monastic orders came after orders of knights, but they developed in tandem with each other as some Knights swore allegiance to the Church, or just nebulously God. From these orders, more orders were created after the knightly orders were split up during a monarchical dispute which saw a huge swath of nobility lose their authority. As time went on, and these monastic orders needed protection from the knights, usually though the Adventurers Guild, the line between the two was hazy since they roughly operated under similar principles and had different vows between orders. By the 4th era, the distinction became even more murky as there technically were no such thing as non-religious orders, and because the church had to more or less wrangle a bunch of different races and former nations under the chaotic theocratic anarcho-communo-libertarian-welfare mess that was "The Empire of Faith", it had no one cultural or institutional set of Vows an order was to adhere to.

As a result, an order may have "Chastity, Poverty, and Obedience" as their vows, while another would have "Defense, Guidance, and Serenity" or "Obedience, Knowledge, Labor" ect.

Usually, you can tell if an order allows marriage if the nuns are allowed to show their hair. Hair is seen as a seductive trait indicative of romance in women in many of the cultures where these orders were founded, and so as culture developed so too did expectations with it. Hence why some priests can marry but others cant depending on the rite.

Not all orders are monastic, but they are all religious orders.
