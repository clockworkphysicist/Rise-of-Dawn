## Agenda

The Illusion of Nuance: In many knightly orders throughout Aldiin, there is a strict code of conduct which outlines the rejection of many mentalities. One such mentalities commonly found in these codes is a rejection of the "Illusion of Nuance". While many view this as an outright rejection of nuance itself, it is ironically far more nuanced than this. The Illusion of Nuance is the view that "The world is not black and white", or more specifically, it is taking morally complex situations and using the complexity of said situations to further a view of moral subjectivism which would in turn excuse evil. Of all of the knightly codes of the 3rd era, the rejection of the Illusion of Nuance is one of the most difficult to fully work through and apply to all facets of knightly life and practice.
