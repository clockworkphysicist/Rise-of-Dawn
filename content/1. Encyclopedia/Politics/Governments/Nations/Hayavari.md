# Hayavari

## History

The land of Hayavari is the home of the Harpies, who are a winged people who delight in their homelands large redwood trees, mountains, valleys, and gales. The harpies live in communities in the trees and in high up perch homes where, from childhood, they perfect the art of flight. Harpies are generally seen as friendly, following what they call the "Tuulen Henki" the Spirit of the wind.In Harpy culture, the movement of wind is, in actuality, the movement of spirits which direct them towards their fates, or warn them of coming danger. However, Hayavari is also home to a good many humans; descendants of the ancient loggers who lived alongside these peoples. Despite their friction, they are brought together by common belief in the god of love, Marion, and the legend of her teaching the ancient harpies how to speak and read.
