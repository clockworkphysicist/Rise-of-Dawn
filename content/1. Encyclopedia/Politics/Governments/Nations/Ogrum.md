## Culture and Traditions

Northerners tend to be a lot less muscular, wear more human or “refined” clothes, and usually will primarily speak common. Southerners are hyper-conservative traditionalists, and will focus more on being physically strong, honor fights, and wearing traditional clothes. They will often, as well, refuse to speak common.

The things Orcs are known for, aside from their rare metals, is their robust martial arts traditions.

## Foods and Cuisine

A big part of orcish cuisine is the abundant orange fruit found dotted along their northern fields; pumpkins.

Orcish food is often overlooked, however their use of pumpkin and squash in dishes is a long-standing culinary tradition. A traditional drink the Orcs make for special celebrations is “Urrak At Krazgran Ahg Bahlghul Kohfi”, or just “Urrak Kohfi” which can be translated into common as “Cream Coffee with Spice of Pumpkin”
