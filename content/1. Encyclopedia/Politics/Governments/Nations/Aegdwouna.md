# Aegdwouna

## History

Aegdwouna is a land of hills, glen, and rocky trenches; a perfect home for the Fey. The Fey are extremely small rabbit like people. In fact, they are about as big as a large rabbit standing on it's hind legs. The Fey are extremely fast, but more then that, they have a unique ability; when they give birth, their children inherit their memories. This makes up for their short lifespan, and means that a Fey will make up to a litter of 5 direct copies of themselves and their lover. However, this process has a limit, and after around 4 generations, the effect stops, and the next generation will not have the memories of their parent. The Fey and the Caoraich were considered centuries long brothers in arms, and as a result the last remaining Caoraich all live in Aegdwouna.
