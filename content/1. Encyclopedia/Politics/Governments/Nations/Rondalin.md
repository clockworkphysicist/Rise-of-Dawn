# Rondalin

## History

The Homeland of the Centaurs, Rondalin, was the frontlines of battle for the lands of Garaputra and Pashuputra during the Grhayuddh. Fought heavily by mercenary corps from Halfaar, the battlefield spanned hundreds of miles of hills and grassland. It was not until, after centuries, the civil war began to become stagnant as the now heavily affected mercenaries began to form their own unique culture and identity. Centaurs had been roaming those lands for millennia, however had never formed a cohesive and organized identity until this war and, supposedly, their encounter with the Halfaar mercenaries.

It is unknown the precise way in which the peoples which we know as centaurs became merged with their horses, as many myths and legends serve different claims, however it was when the Kingdom of Garaputra declared war on their former allies that the centaurs became well and truly independent. Centaurs are careful; cautious by nature. Unlike what many know the Halfaar Berserkers for, Centaurs are methodical and calculating, moving slow when necessary and striking fast and decisively. It is perhaps because of this that they have a reputation as a reserved, yet direct people.
