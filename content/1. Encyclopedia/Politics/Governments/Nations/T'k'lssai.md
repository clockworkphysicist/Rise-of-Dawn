# T'k'lssai

## History

T'k'lissai is the land of the driders; a kingdom of cobwebs and ancient citadels which sits along a vast river and a sprawling forest of dead woods. The Driders are a reclusive, pacifistic people, however they are virtually impossible to invade due to the webbed nature of their home, and the native hostility towards invaders who they consider "Prey".
