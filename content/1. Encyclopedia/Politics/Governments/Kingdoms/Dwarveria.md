## Culture and Traditions

In Dwarven culture, they do not drink recreationally that often, at least no more than their neighboring races, but during ceremonies, festivals, and community gatherings, they drink as a custom regardless of the occasion. Dwarves, however, like to drink slowly and in moderation; pacing themselves. Here's the problem, while many may know of Dwarven mead, few have actually had it. It's actually not that alcoholic at all, if anything they consider it a sweet treat. They don't drink mead at their ceremonies, they drink something called Rakfiskøl, which is a traditional ale made out of grain and fermented fish which is just a small 192 Proof alcohol. They can handle their liquor well, but, again they drink it at a steady pace. There is an idiom in Dwarven culture for how long a party should go: "Until the cat dies." Given that all at a party, man, woman, and child of any age, all partake in drinking, the idea that this idiom may come from an actual occurrence isn't unthinkable.
