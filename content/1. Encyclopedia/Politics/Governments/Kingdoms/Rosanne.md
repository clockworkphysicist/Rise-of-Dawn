## Foods and Cuisine

Rosanne, in the 3rd Era, was not around for very long; in fact it was the annexation of Rosanne by the Confederacy which eventually led to the reestablishment of the Kingdom of Carynth, but in that time alcohol was indeed still very popular, despite a majority of drinks having exceedingly low alcohol content during this time period.

In Southern Rosanne, the most common drink of choice was Beer; a staple of Southern Rosanian culture. The most famed beer from the area was Rosafarbenes Lagerbier, which was pressed and fermeted with rose pedals mixed in with the hops in a unique fashion. This led to a slightly floral taste which could be difficult at first to get accustomed to, but was supposedly phenomenal with a fresh pretzel dipped into it.

In the north, their culture was more heavily formed by the Exalic peoples who settled the area centuries ago, who famously were not often affected by alcohol, but did often drink and produce wine. Their most famous drink was Vin de la Mer Bleue, a type of wine which had a slight bluish tint to it, similar to Kataeli blueberry wine. Where this wine differed, however, was that it had a more pronounced floral taste with a deeper and more bitter dark grape.

Of course, a drink which was prized for both cultures, and tied them more broadly to historical Carynth, was "Aselwen Gold", a rare type of alcohol which was described as being much like a honied butterscotch wine in it's taste, with foaminess of a beer, and texture of a mead. It is said that when Queen Nicadea the Beloved negotiated the end of the war with the royal family of Rosanne, she served this spirit as a sign of unity. The royal families first drink was known as 'the sip that ended a kingdom', with the second drink being known as 'the sip that started anew'.
