# Oreyen

## History

The Kingdom of Oreyen is known as the Land of the Ancients or "The Land of Endless Autumn" for it's year round autumnal forests and it's strict adherence to ancient traditions. Once one of the largest empires in the Eastern lands, Oreyen's Pharaoh looks to the sun to ascertain truths of the ancient past, handed down from the gods and whispered by spirits of the honored dead.

When the Oreyenic people pass on, their copper skin hardens to that of a statue. Their burial rites involve their bodies being melted down, forged into armor, weapons, or even the walls and streets of their cities. In this way, they believe that they are surrounded by the spirits of their honored dead always.
