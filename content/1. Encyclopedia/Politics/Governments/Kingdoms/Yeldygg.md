## Culture and Traditions

In impic culture, gold is considered something sacred and treasured beyond its material and economic value, as to Imps only a select few may handle gold coinage, and to gaze upon unrefined gold is considered unclean unless one is a holy person, of which their smiths are considered. This is scarcely surprising, as the golden jewelry they wear is not merely decorative, but to them each piece is believed to be an extension of the imp itself. For a female, the belly ring is her ability to rear legitimate children, rings a marriage and bond to family, earrings their connection to their ancestors, and every imp of age wears a chest piece indicating their personhood; indeed each piece is vital to them, and to remove an imps gold is considered by them to be worse than death, and to attempt to re-equip it is considered a grave disgrace and the highest dishonesty.
