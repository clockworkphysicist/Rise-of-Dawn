# Illydar

## History

Illydar was a kingdom that excelled in mystical sorceries, and became so advanced that they created a series of portals all across Aldiin which they used to connect back with the lost Elven tribes of the Morning and Evening (The Exalics and Olterics). Being masters of magic, however, they had a bad habit of delving too deep into forbidden arts, and this led to different factions which all devoted themselves to different sorceries, and one which devoted themselves to the spirits of nature, secluding themselves off to their temples in the Olteric Lands. All of this came at a price, as the sorcerers of Illydar noticed that the metaphysical plane was becoming unstable, threatening to tear the world apart and plunge Illydar into the sea. In response to this, the prince of Illydar traveled the land seeking out enough power to reclaim the thrones of Illuen from the four elven kingdoms and restore balance to the magical world.
