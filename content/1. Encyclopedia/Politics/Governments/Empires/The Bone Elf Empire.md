## Geography

Covered in soot and ash; not lonly highly volcanic, but actually also has an extremely rare type of magma which can actually kill elves as though it were were normal lava to a human. The other thing it's known for is having the bones of thousands of dead creatures, dragons primarily. It is the land where Leo first struck Aldiin, after all. The bones are so abundant that there is one area where there is a forest made of them. With that said, there are also many verdant hot springs and calderas with cool wind and alpine snows.
