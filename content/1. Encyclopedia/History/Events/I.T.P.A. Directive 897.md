# Event

## History

The League of Nations Anomaly Administration Bureau had, during the twilight years of the early 7th era, taken great lengths to contain and research individuals whose risk classification far exceeded anything that the LoN had the capacity to deal with. Individuals whose powers, left unchecked, may develop to nigh apocalyptic proportions. In accordance with Directive 897 of the ITPA, a bio-research company, Alumaida Solutions, was hired to study anomalous individuals who were captured and neutralized.

A string of containment failures, new potential research opportunities, and severe project oversights led to further research efforts being put on indefinite hiatus. The most controversial of these research facilities, located in an abandoned wing of the Memorial Hospital Behavior and Mental Wing. This location was created in order to contain and experiment on anomalous juveniles.

Though the full records are still being ascertained, the currently known subjects are:

Subject 321 'Psycho-Breaker': Subject appears to have extremely powerful telekinetic and reality warping abilities, including levitation, reality bending, and psionic blasts. Must be monitored constantly and kept in a state of limited lucidity in an experimental 'meta-sleep chamber'.

Subject 413 'Little Albert': Subject has the ability to induce extreme hallucinogenic fear into individuals when in a heightened emotional state. Appears to have a level of reality warping effects which ensure the enforcement of said fear. Kept under constant sedation, electric shock applied for experimentation. Containment measures include the administration of an audio/visual medium from document 9d-Walden.

Subject 808 'Jishin': Subject can cause earthquakes at a range of 2.0 to (Data Expunged for Security Under Directive 897-02) upon impact with their hands or feet on the ground. Subject is to be encased in an apparatus to induce immobility and promote muscular atrophy.
