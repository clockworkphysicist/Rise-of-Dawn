# Event

## History

The Great Crusade during the middle of the 4th era ended with the Church awakening the Arch Dragon of Eternity in the Dragon Isle and, effectively, flying it at near relativistic speeds over the maw of inferno; completely destroying and ripping the inferno in half and separating the Ashkazi peninsula from the rest of Aldiin. This, in turn, caused magic to be dampened, and no longer as easy to use as it had been in millennia prior. The liminal energy which had long since blanketed Aldiin was now concentrated in pockets, and now only people with the most powerful of magic coursing through their blood could access it with ease.
