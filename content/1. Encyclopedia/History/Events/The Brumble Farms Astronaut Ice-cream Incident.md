# Event

## History

In the 8th era, Brumble Farms sponsored a launch of a space station made of astronaut ice-cream to advertise a new flavor and make the claim that astronauts DO eat ice cream in space. As soon as the station left orbit the lack of gravity caused the interior to crumble apart, leading to the entire station being filled with particles and dust; compromising the oxygen system. The crew had to be rescued by a launch crew from Eldius-1 and the station was abandoned. Ironically, this debate kickstarted one of the most important scientific achievements in aerotech; artificial gravity. Funded, researched, and achieved by Brumble Farms Dairy Company. The reality that it was Brumble Farms who successfully achieved this scientific marvel was the source of ire in the scientific community.
