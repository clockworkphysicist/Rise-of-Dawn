# Event

## History

One of the key driving factors behind the Corporate Wars was the open letter that Free Market United had sent to the League of Nations, demanding that signatory countries enact into law a resolution drafted by the conglomerate called the "Capital Acquisition Assurance Act." This proposed act stated that members of Free Market United's corporate group would be exempt from any regulations and from the legal requirement of consumer consent. This included rights to advertisement, rights to products post-purchase, and the right to forcibly acquire investments from citizens to ensure stable profits (known as the Prosperity Tax).

Both the open letter and the resolution were rejected and denounced as "fucking psychotic," quoting the Secretary-General.
