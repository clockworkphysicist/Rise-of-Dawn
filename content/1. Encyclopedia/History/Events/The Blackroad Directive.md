# Event

## History

The Blackroad Directive was a multi-national industrial development plan instituted near the start of the 5th era where a collective of national parties contracted new companies to take land in the free lands to develop, who then sub-contracted wealthy Iron Elf manufacturers to export their materials/management capacity to actively run these new factories. One of the first and largest of these "Factory Cities" was The Oldstone Industrial Plant, which is devoted to processing and manufacturing; working in chemicals and metals. This was the freelands until the Great War, when these industrial areas became prime targets for bombing raids and all out war between different factions.
