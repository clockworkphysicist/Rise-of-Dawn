# Event

## History

By the middle 3rd Era, the clans of Tk'lk had become ravaged by inter-clan conflict, decades of severe famine, and most importantly, the death of Hive Queen Jazrii during a sudden and bloody Voranian hunt. Faced with the potential end of their homeland, many Tk'lk had fled to the northern lands, seeking refuge in colonies in Tezralan Bay. It was here that the Dark Swarm would find it's beginnings.

With the Illythids of Tok-Brokko exiting a vicious civil war with victory in the hands of the Abyss Master Tabraaak Onzoth, the Illythids had become the preeminent power of the western Ashkazi peninsula; receiving the undisputed title of the Masters of the Deep. Using this power, Tok-Brokko had founded a new capitol hub in the center of Tezralan bay known as Kor-Zolan "The Twisted Spire", where they could focus on unifying and delving into the deepest depths of Eldrich power. It was with this that more and more falling kingdoms of the peninsula began to pledge fealty to the Abyss Master, becoming vassals under a singular unified empire; "The Dark Lands".

The crowning jewel of this new empire was the capstone of Eldrich research and worship that had been culminating for millennia, the discovery of the bottom of the depths. The very thing that had driven the Ashkazi peoples since time immemorial. The Center of the Old Ones power... the Void, along with it's master and creator; the Dusk Lord. Gaining power from the pure truth of Eldrich power, the Dark Lands tapped into the Dusk Lords void and found new purpose and direction... and the scattered Insectoids a new hive mind to bow to. A new monarch to guide their hand. Those Tk'lk who did not remain in the Dark Lands had long since fled to the emerging Greater Ascalonian Empire... and those who remained were forged with new power. Abilities allowing them to travel through the void in a hive of nothingness, communicate through the deep... and in a Swarm of Darkness they found purpose.
