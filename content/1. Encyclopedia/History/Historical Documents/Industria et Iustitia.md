# Industria et Iustitia

## Background

## History

## Significance and Impact

Widely considered to be 'the proclamation which doomed Ascalon'.

## Content

"Let it be known: It is in the judgement of the holy church, by divine edict of the pontiff and authority given by the lord our God, that any act that is done, be it industrial or societal, which is not in accordance with his will or law be anathema. Any process which undermines the sanctity of life or dignity of creation shall not be tolerated, no matter what benefits, profits, or advancement it may bring. It is better to act in accordance with that which is righteous and suffer inefficiency and difficulty than to acquiesce to injustice and obtain expedience and convenience."
