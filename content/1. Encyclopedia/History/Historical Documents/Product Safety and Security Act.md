# Product Safety and Security Act

## Background

The Product Safety and Security Act of 6E 875 requires all products sold in signatory League of Nations countries to be properly certified for safety and functionality in accordance with the Surgeon General recommendations from the signatory nations health and wellness agencies. With this in mind, a product may prove itself as safe for consumption or use through a trial period outside of access to citizens of each country. This clause is what allows companies to test their products in the D.E.Z. without regulation, as residents of the D.E.Z. are not citizens.

Coincidentally, this is not a concern for most national health agencies, as there is an inexplicable trend for products to never leave the trial phase of their development, preventing any dangers to national health and wellbeing.
