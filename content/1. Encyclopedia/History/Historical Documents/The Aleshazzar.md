# The Aleshazzar

## Background

The Aleshazzar is a book on the conduct of effective statecraft from Oreyen. The most controversial, yet most widely utilized contribution from the book is the policy of "A Summan" which reads "A matur e meshem harrashar akshekin. A matur e jemut harrashar summan." which translates to "In a war of diplomacy, attack the soldiers. In a war of ideology, attack the civilians." this coincided with an earlier passage stating "Any war against the peoples of the hills (this is an ancient term used by Oreyenic people to refer to foreign people in general) must be carried out to exterminate as many of their race as possible, and as violently as possible so that they may never forget the pain, and thus will either not rise against you again, or can be manipulated by their revanchism."
