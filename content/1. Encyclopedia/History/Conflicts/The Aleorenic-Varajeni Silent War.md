# The Aleorenic-Varajeni Silent War

The Aleorenic-Varajeni Silent War of the late 2nd era is considered one of the most difficult wars to study because it was simultaneously a conflict officially reported to have zero casualties, yet it truthfully stood as one of the most one-sided military humiliations in Aldinic history. According to all official records, this war was declared by Basileus Narcencion Dinopolyus. Three years later, with no military action on either side, the king revoked his declaration. This led to a state of confusion across the various kingdoms, prompting Narcencion to clarify that trade relations had stabilized and war wasn't tenable due to Oreyen's refusal of military access.

In truth, known only to the soldiers who fought and the courts of the high nobility, Narcencion had dispatched an army of around 20,000 near Oreyen through the Freelands territory to assault the eastern border of Varaje. Anticipating this move, and possessing one of the greatest wealth bases in Aldiin, the Sultan had hired over 30,000 mercenaries from over 400 different mercenary groups and deployed them to launch a surprise assault from the hills of Ethumet. Caught completely unaware and unprepared for such an assault, the Aleorenic army was routed, its soldiers splintering off and unable to reunite. Upon learning of the large counterforce that had attacked his soldiers, the Basileus began rallying a second army for a renewed assault, only to discover that multiple bandit raids had broken out across the country along different trade routes. Realizing exactly what had happened, Basileus Narcencion halted merchant trade through the land, dispatching battalions to suppress "bandit" activity. In response, Varaje opened up a new trade route around Aleoren through the ruins of Lunavale via an agreement with Legatus Morosus of the Ascalonian Empire, further crippling the Aleorenic economy. After nearly two years of chasing off bandits and suffering blows to their coffers, Aleoren had no choice but to concede.
