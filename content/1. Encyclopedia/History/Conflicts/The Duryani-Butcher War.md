# The Duryani-Butcher War

The Duryani-Butcher War was, in essence, a three year long conflict between the Duryani People's Republic and the Scarlet Butcher Gang of West Alinton at the start at the 7th Era. The war resulted in the shocking downfall of the Duryani People's Republic, and the establishment of the Scarlet Butchers as the pre-eminent street gang in Western Alinton and the WA-ITPA territories before the rise of the Unholy Qabalah.

The war was notable for being one of the primary motivating factors for the League of Nations Anomaly Administration Bureau to create and standardize the "Anomalous Risk Classification of Individuals" system, extending it from the prior ARC systems put in place during the 5th and 6th eras. To quote the Director General of the LNAAB during it's creation: "Though such a system of policing and monitoring may seem draconian in nature, witnessing a group of indestructible psychotic cannibals get high on crack and systematically butcher the entire politburo of a sovereign nation more than warrants it in our departments humble opinion."

The DPR was a failing authoritarian country, so it's downfall wasn't the shocking part, it was that it was a "common" street gang is what did it in and just... fucked off afterwards for lack of a better term. It started when a Scarlet Butcher was kidnapped and killed after he released info on politically sensitive information. To the Scarlet Butchers it was literally just a "blood for blood" exchange, they really didn't care that it was against a state entity.

The DPR then split apart and was re-annexed by it's neighboring states, as the political identity and will of the civilians there had been looong broken by the corrupt government. On a grand scale, IE for most of the rest of the world, the more important factor was that someone can just walk up to a small army and demolish it for no other reason than that they are in an altered mental state and therefore a different level of power from the average person was terrifying.

The effects of "Ascension" or "The Crazy Factor" was something that had been known about for literal millennia, and yet it never was a mainstream known thing until the drug epidemic and, beyond that, gangs began to form around these powerful individuals in the wake of the Corporate Wars. Even then the extent and nature of these abilities are obscure to 98% of the population, who only has the general idea that "Sometimes people can just take a bullet to the head and keep moving because they're just built different I guess."
The other shocking thing was that the raw brutality of some of these gangs were put on display, and the fact that many now had high grade military equipment at their disposal after the corporate wars made a lot of people go from seeing the DEZ as nothing more than just "The Backwash of the Civilized World" to seeing it as a place where a lot of the hard power on Aldiin was starting to be concentrated.
