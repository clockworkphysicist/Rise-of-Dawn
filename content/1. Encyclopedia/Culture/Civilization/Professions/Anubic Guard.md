# Anubic Guard

## Description

## Purpose and Responsibilities

Some are in fact released and they pass on, others roam the forests as wandering guardians of the spirits of their ancestors. Either way, despite being in theory immortal, Anubi Guards do one day, when all of their duties are fulfilled and all of the spirits are satisfied, peacefully doze off into death.

## History

The Anubic Guard are royal/tomb tower guards for Oreyen. Considered elite lifeforms, they do not need to eat or sleep, and are nigh indestructible. Though it was once considered a great honor to become an Anubic Huard, the cost to the individual is so great that, often, the Oreyenics need to promise a vast amount of riches/privileges in order to get anyone to willingly become one, with there said to be only one created every three hundred years when the moons align. The process for their creation is extremely painful, where the initiate must swallow a jade scarab from their temple, giving them their indestructability as the scarab becomes part of the initiates body. They then are filled with molten rubber before being placed into a golden sarcophagus, which is then filled with a sort of silicone jelly like a mold.

The entire body is coated with another layer of rubber which is cooled quickly to act as a sort of "outer skin" as the sarcophagus is then squished into shape under an extreme ammount of pressure. The enchanted scarab maintains the body of the initiate, taking the rubber, jackal shaped the outer body created by the process and integrating it into itself. With a hiss of steam, the process ends, and the Anubic Guard rises from it's sarcophagus. Through the power of Bal-Atun, they are made effectively indestructible, immortal, and unrelenting. They do not, usually, have mouths, but by focusing they can manifest them in order to bite/snarl if necessary. This renders them completely mute without the use of magic.

They are given immortality in exchange for a life of servitude to the mausoleums and, as well, a stunting of their emotions and personality. They are entombed in large, lumbering bodies of rubber and gel, which effectively acts as a pliable, but indestructible body which they pilot; the body eventually just becoming part of them over time.
