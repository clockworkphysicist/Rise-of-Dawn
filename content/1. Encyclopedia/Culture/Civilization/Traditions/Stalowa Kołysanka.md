## Execution

Stalowa Kołysanka, the Lullaby of Blades. A form of ritual duel from the north in which two duelists with serrated sabers aim for each others necks, aiming to fight to the death. Often, the duel will occur where both duelists lock blades for hours or even days; but a few centimeters of distance meaning life or death. The duel cannot be stopped with honor preserved unless a maiden plucks a red rose from the forest and offers it up.

It is from this tradition that Rose Maidens, girls who roam the forests with wicker baskets of roses, first came about. These Rose Maidens would eventually evolve over time to become the origins of the Order of the Red Rose.
