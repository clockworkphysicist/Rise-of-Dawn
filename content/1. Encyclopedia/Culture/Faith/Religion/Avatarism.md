## Mythology

Avatars will have three forms, one that looks like what they looked like on the mortal realm, one which looks like a cosmic being that they represent, and one which is almost like the perfect thusness of a thing; unable to be seen or beheld by the mortal mind
Each state of their being is a different distance away from the platonic perfect of a thing, because the farther they are from imperfection in embodying the thusness of a thing, the less they can be expressed in it's material reality. Because the material world can only glimpse at, but never experience anything more than a reflection of a perfect thing.

An avatar in its spiritual form is outside of time, experiencing past, present, and future all as “thus”. However, they have no mind as they have no “self”, they can only “be”. In its realm, it’s like a separate plane of reality; knowing all that pertains to it in the hypothetical and actual sense, but having a mind and sense of being. Then, lastly, it can manifest in the physical world both in life (before it obtains its avatar state) and after ascension (as a manifestation). This manifestation, or living avatar, is a physical being that can interact with reality, though it’s actions will always be impermanent. It will have its own mind and knowledge, but know only that which pertains to it in the immediate sense. The three forms of the avatar do not share knowledge between each other, and in this sense are of separate “soul” but containing one “spirit”.
Keeping in mind a soul being one’s consciousness and their spirit being the metaphysical “essence” that they are.
