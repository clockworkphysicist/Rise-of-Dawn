# The Maiden and the Blade

## Summary

The story speaks of an Elven Princess who is told by a soothsayer that her tyranny against the Fey and her destruction of the Fairies would lead to her losing her head in a duel, and the soothsayer tells her all of the signs that will happen before then.

The Princess mocks the prophecy and the idea that a mere Fey could harm her, as only a magic sword could. The poem describes a young Fey girl standing up to her and challenging her to a duel in her elderly fathers stead, and the Fey girl uses the sword he got from the Fairy of the Lake because it is her fathers lucky sword. The Fey tells her that should she go in peace, and declare she yields, then the Princess would be spared that day, and the Princess laughs. The Princess is cut upon the hand and realizes that the cut isn't healing, and then slowly begins to panic as she notices the signs one after another, but her arrogance and pride prevent her from yielding, until the Fey strikes true and cuts her head from her shoulders.
