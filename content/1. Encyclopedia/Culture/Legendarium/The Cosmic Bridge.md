# The Cosmic Bridge

## Summary

The Cosmic Bridge is a road made of hardened light which appears as a rainbow. Legends say that such a rainbow will sometimes appear behind mysterious doors in places they aught not to be, and at the end is a gazebo affixed on a small island floating amongst the stars.
