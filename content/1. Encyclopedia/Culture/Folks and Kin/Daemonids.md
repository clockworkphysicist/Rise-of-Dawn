# Daemonids

## Description

The Daemonids were once people of other races, but during the Churches holy war against inferno whenever the Dark Army captured commanders or particularly virtuous people they’d turn them into peoples stereotypical image of  Demons before either breaking their minds or just releasing them in their corrupted form. The Church ended up classifying them as a protected people because citizens kept making lynch mobs to hunt them down, and as a result the Daemonids became an official race. They're similar to imps in that they can come in any color or pattern (in fact their similarity to imps may have been one of the bigger causes of prejudice than the religious reasons in many parts of the world), but really they appear greatly like the counterparts of the race they were once, or in the case of Daemonids this far ahead, their ancestors were once.

Some have unique attributes that look more horrific, but these are usually due to them being descendants of Daemonids who were corrupted to serve a specific function in the Dark Army. There are different classifications of demon, with lesser ones having manifested forms and greater ones being immaterial. Daemonids aren't either, instead just being people who have been warped to fit in with the cultural stereyotype of "Horned, Bright Skinned tricksters/seducers". The Church had an extremely strong theology, and as such they didn't fall for it, but the general populace? VERY effective psychological warfare. To them, every taste is the most tantalizing food they've ever eaten, every sensual touch orgasmic, every sound like an orchestra. But any sense of spiritual fulfillment from the material world is completely hollow, and they lack the perception of sublimity.
There's often a sense of disconnect with something deeper within enjoyment of anything. Something inherently human that, even without experiencing it, you feel you're missing. Something you can only feel in dreams.

It would be as though one never feels the inexpressible feeling you get when you look at beauty, the feeling of creating something that has been in your imagination, or the feeling of sublimity you get from being in the presence of something awe inspiring. A state where meditation and spiritual peace cause physical pain or agitation rather than fulfillment, but the knowledge given by reason that those things are what life was made for.

They know it's real, but they can't reach it; it's an inherent feeling of being existentially incomplete. Their only glimpse at the feeling is in dreams, which fade when they wake up.
