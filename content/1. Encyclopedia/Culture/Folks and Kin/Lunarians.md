# Lunarians

## Description

The Lunarians are an Exalic race who exist in the Empire as an extremely small minority. Natives of the Kingdom of Lunaria, they are an Exalic people who have light seafoam mint colored skin and seafoam green hair. Though they had a kingdom of their own which they carved out from the Ashes of the Ancient Ascalonian Empire, their people were subjugated during the early 2nd era, and over the course of history their identity as a nation disappeared. The kingdom they are from, meanwhile, was a place in Ascalon in which the night is eternal and the moon always shines.
