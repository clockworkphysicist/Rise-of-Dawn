## Biological Information

The Ascalonians suffer something called the Ashen curse, which as a result of the Apocalypsis meant:

1. All Ascalonians outside of a 50 mile radius of Sacramentum turned to ash.
2. Ascalonians within the radius were now directly tied to the wellbeing of their empire: if the Empire died conceptually, they all died with it literally. In times of hardship, they would suffer malady.
3. All Ascalonians who break the laws of the Empire within the Empire fall out of communion with the Empire, and begin to experience maladies in accordance to the severity of the crime.
4. Any Ascalonian that leaves the boundaries of their Empire, the border, will turn to ash slowly.
5. The ruler of Ascalon is now physically incapable of breaking the laws of the Empire which they devote. They are incapable of manipulating or acting on attempts to challenge this whatsoever.

The First Empress would act as the catalyst of the binding between the Avatar of Time and the Empire; her and her descendants would be given the means to imbue the essence of the Empire into crystals they can create, and as a contingency she would act as an anchor for the avatar. Only the emperor can create a tear once every month. Tears have traditionally been given to Ascalonian Knights in order to expand the empire and act as community leaders, who would become Grand Masters and then Senators, passing on their position, and tear, to their next of kin. After the fall of the Senate, the emperor reclaimed the tears and started to distribute them amongst arc-beurocrats, Inquisitorial Operatives, and civilian traders in order to increase diplomatic and structural outreach. When their mission or term is completed with the tear, the recipient is expected to return it to Sacramentum promptly or face damnation via exclusion. An Ascalonian can live for about 10 years outside of the borders of the empire, 5 if 1000 miles away from the border.

If the Emperor is not in power, or the collective belief is that he is not in power, the ashen curse will kick in regardless of proximity to the empire. Those within sacramentum can never experience the ashen curse apart from falling out of communion via criminal activity. The Emperor can not commit any crimes, and any attempt to subvert this will result in the emperor being incapable of carrying out the subversion. Any law that the emperor is able to freely break is, by its nature, not a true law. Two things are needed for an individual to be considered a part of emperor:

1. Direct blood connection to the first emperor.
2. Common belief in the legitimacy of the emperor

The bloodline must be direct. A cousin or niece of the current emperor will not count, and as such they are only bound as a member of the royalty, though in Ascalonian culture this means little functionally, and over time unless breeding with the imperial line the blood relation will fade. Should there be no compatible male descendant for a female descendant to breed with, any male will suffice, but they will not be considered emperor. There will always be a female descendant living. The female descendant will be reincarnated at conception, starting with the current emperor. If there is no valid recipient for the first empress in the imperial line, then the child will move to a member of the royal family, if there are no royal family members valid to conceive, then it will be transferred to any Ascalonian, if there are no Ascalonians left, then any race may conceive of the empress.

So, in essence the Ascalonian imperial structure MUST preclude that:

1. They are incapable of breaking laws, and cannot change laws to nullify or negate the founding laws.
2. They must have direct blood-blood inbreeding or else their bloodline will decay and so will the lives of millions of people.
3. The Emperors must have complete and absolutist control over the Empire or else the entire Ascalonian race will begin to die.
4. They are destined to, at some point, bear a child who is the reincarnation of the first Empress, who is cursed to not only be unkillable before a set amount of time, but also anchors the eternal curse to their whole empire.
