# Orcs

## Description

For tusks, northerners tend to either have none, or have a sharp lower canines. Southerners tend to have larger tusks and can have one or two. That said, they are generally somewhat painful as they are similar to wisdom teeth. They will often chew on wooden sticks or pumpkin vines to keep them healthy and smooth aches.
