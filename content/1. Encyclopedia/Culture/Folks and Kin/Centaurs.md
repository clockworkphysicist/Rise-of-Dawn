# Centaurs

## Description

Common traits are that they are mostly brown skinned, have standard human hair colors (except for white and blood red), and have equine ears. They look semi-exalic facially, but their culture seems to be a mix between Noregir and Garahatma.

## History

Many millennia ago, during the 1st Era, Animalia, Rondalin, and a country now in the confederacy were all one kingdom; Garahatma. However, due to a civil war on the basis of breakdown between the brahmin of the south and the raj of the north, there was a civil war which broke the country in half. The centaurs were an elite warrior class of mercenaries from the lands of Halfaar who, over multiple generations, adopted the Animalian practices of warfare and social structure and implemented it into their culture. However, being loyal to the Raj, the mercenaries rejected the philosophies and religion of the brahmin, many of their ranks still holding their ties to their homelands in the north.

Thus, by the time the civil war essentially ended, the centaurs had reclaimed the western half of the country, bringing in more mercenaries and warrior clans over time. Once the confederacy had taken over their employer, and with no contracts to fulfill, they simply held the land and refused to leave. Over centuries, the war was forgotten, though their "mission" endures.
