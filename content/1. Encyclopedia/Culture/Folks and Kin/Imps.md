# Imps

## Description

For many, there is no tale more heart wrenching than the fate of the Imps of Yeldygg; the lost kingdom. The Impic people once had a powerful Kingdom in the south of Aldiin known as Yeldygg, where it was believed that they had mastered a mysterious and powerful form of magic based on the sun, moon, and stars. However, near the end of the 1st Era, an event known as the Yeldyggan genocide resulted in their kingdoms annihilation and what many thought to be the extermination of their race.

Though they are still around, they are a people without a homeland, travelling in nomadic caravans around Aldiin and forming small tight knit communities; the only settlement they have permanently is within the walls of the capitol of Imbvara. As a result they are looked down on as being a "dirty" race; seen as swindlers, freeloaders and thieves. However, despite all this, they still have memories of their lost kingdom which they pass down through stories from generation to generation.

Imps skin can be of any color or pattern, and along with this, they may have horns and a tail, only horns or a tail, or neither, and their eye color and sclera can be any combination of colors. Imps carry with them the legacy of Yeldyyg, and in many ways the homeland that they lost serves as one of the driving forces of their cultural tradition. Imps are experts at making their home wheresoever they may roam, and as a result one can find Imps to be surprisingly proficient homemakers. Whether it be an abandoned castle or a soaked cloth propped up by a stick, Imps make the best of wherever they lay their heads, and often share their comfort and comradery with those they travel with. Just as well, Imps tend to be well traveled, and experienced in various different labors and crafts by necessity. As a result, they have an advantage on most any menial labor which they set their mind too.
