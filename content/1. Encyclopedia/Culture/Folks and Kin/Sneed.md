## Description

Short creatures. About 1.5 feet tall, and with pale skin. They as well, don't appear to have mouths, but do have tiny beady black eyes and a pigs snout they communicate with through little "honks". All other information aside from this is unknown, as they wear what appear to be tiny hooded robes and can somehow quickly walk away faster than anyone can approach them. They appear to have time based powers to some extent.
