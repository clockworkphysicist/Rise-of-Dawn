# Giants

## Description

Giants are gigantic, imposing beings of usually about 30-40 feet in height. They are somewhat slender, and tend to have a clay like tan skin and hair. For the most part, they are silent, but what is most striking about them is their extreme speed and agility. Most giants, however, use enchanted golems which they channel their consciousness and spirit into in order to carry out tasks at the size of other races. These golems act as vessels which they pilot and communicate through, and depending on the magic level of the giant crafting it, the golem may be either extremely rudimentary clay pottery beings, or very lifelike and like miniature versions of themselves.

They arent very strong at all relatively speaking. Their bodies tend to be rather frail, which they make up for with blinding speed. With that said, they do have the ability to project their consciousness into the spirits of things and possess inanimate objects. They also are immmune to radiation poisoning, as their diet is often high in radioactive elements and minerals.
