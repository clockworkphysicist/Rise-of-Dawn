# Kataeli

## Description

The Kataeli are the people of the mountainous region of Katael, placed between the Human Confederacy and the Ascalonian Empire. The Kataeli, while still tall, are noticeably shorter than many of the other Exalic races, however their facial features and general bone structure make them unmistakable as such. What is striking about their kind, meanwhile, is that while they have a somewhat ashen red color for their hair, many you may meet can have hair of any color on the rainbow, much like an elf. This is not through biology, however, as once a student of the Grand College of Dechestus comes of age, their hair is enchanted in order to give a mark of individuality, but unity with the culture of the city-state. The color of their hair, traditionally, is changed to match their master, school of study, or family lineage, however it’s not uncommon to even have it just be personal preferences.
