## History

The Palisade hotel was once a glittering gem in the middle of eastern Salva, it’s 50 floors accommodated people of all races and status as they would drive for miles to experience what was once the city of Montyvale and, of course, the beauty of the ancient Ruthlaine Cathedral. After the compound outbreaks of the Kulla Virus and a nuclear meltdown at the Omega 6 facility nearby, the hotel quickly became one of the last signs of civilization in the area, which had fallen to ruins. Each floor is covered with graffiti, mold, and remnants of peoples livelihoods as they scrambled to escape the city; those who were lucky enough. Now, the Hotel houses but two things; small enclaves of people who still live in the few habitable floors which they maintain, and degenerate souls who slaughter any who dare walk through those decrepit halls.
