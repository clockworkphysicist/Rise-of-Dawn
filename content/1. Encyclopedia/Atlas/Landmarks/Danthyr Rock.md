## History

At the end of the 4th era, the Great Awakening had led to civil wars all over the Empire, and part of the reason it was so difficult to navigate was that individual communities could proclaim that they were not involved in the war, and the church would allow them to not be called to produce assets for the war. However, this often backfired as those communities would often be seen as easy pickings by the revolutionaries and the church would have to come in and take it back at a disadvantage. An example of this is the battle for Danthyr, where the human contingent managed to take 99% of the city, but the remaining forces fled to an off shore mountain and burned the docks to cover their escape. There were multiple attempts to siege it, but since it was a fortified monastery, it was not only difficult to breach, but as it’s the resting place of a beloved saint, it was also a VERY unpopular target. As a result, once independence was declared, Danthyr Rock is still one of the furthest borders of the Ascalonian Empire, and by the 7th era a popular tourist spot.
