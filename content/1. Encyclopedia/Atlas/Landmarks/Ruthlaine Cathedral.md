## History

Ruthlaine Cathedral was an important pilgrimage spot for many worshipers in the 4th era, however when capital and tourism began to flood into Montyvale, the cathedrals sacred presence had become drowned out by the hustle and bustle of the modern world. Leagues of worshippers had been traded for seas of tourists, and over time, Ruthlaine had been forgotten by the wider Pastoral world. Once the dual cataclysms of Montyvale took place, however, Ruthlaine was one of the three buildings in Montyvale still standing after so many thousands of years. To this day, its deaconess still roams the halls maintaining the run down cathedral as best she can, and the priest of Montyvale sits quietly at the chair of the altar, awaiting any worshipers brave enough to come and receive his blessing.
