# Dral'Serar

## Description

The Grand Monastery of Dral'Serar is the main hub of all of the different orders throughout the Empire, and acts as the central nexus by which inner Ascalon is connected to the rest of the church. Here, Monastic Orders, Sororitas Convents, and the Knights of the Church along with the Arch-Deacons of the respective dioceses, convene to discuss goings on, report on the well being of the empire, and call upon the Inquisition when direct intervention by the magisterium is needed. From this place, the recommendations and direct needs from the lay trusteeship is presented for consideration and discussion before, inevitably, filed for review to the central Ecclesiarchic Administration. Meanwhile, most law and theological doctrine that is implemented and taught throughout the Ecclesiastical Empire is done directly in the Capitol of Sacramentum itself, where once every five years the arch-bishops of the Empire meet for an Ecumenical Council, ten representatives from Dral'Serar being in attendance to discuss the findings and concerns of the low clergy and lay faithful.
