# Inferno

## History

The Inferno. Possibly the most accursed, horrid place on Aldiin. It is the location where the veil between the physical and metaphysical was torn, and the chaotic emotions of all life manifested into raw, infernal, animalistic evil. It is a land where demons spawn from the gaping maw left behind by sheer hatred and rage, the rivers flow with blood, and the land is made of pulsating gore and raw, condensed agony.
