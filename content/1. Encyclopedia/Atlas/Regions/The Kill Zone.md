# The Kill Zone

## History

When the rift of Inferno Opened, the Killzone was the site of a massive, greuling, all encompassing battle fought between the Exalic Elves and their enemies; the Voranians. A shockwave of magic killed all of the soldiers, but kept their spirits bound to the mortal realm, where they are forced to fight for all of eternity in a never-ending battle where they repeat the same actions as the day of their death.
