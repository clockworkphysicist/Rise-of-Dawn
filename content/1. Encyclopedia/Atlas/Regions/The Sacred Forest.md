# The Sacred Forest

## History

Just past Aegdwouna is the Sacred Forest; the land the Fey cite as the source of their peoples magical attunement. The sacred forest is another heavily liminal space on Aldiin, and is a peninsula with nothing more than an empty forest, it's trees perfectly lined in straight rows. However, the trees cannot be destroyed; in fact it seems as though one can weave in and out of this land and the only thing they'll hear aside from the noises they make is the chirping of cicadas. At the very tip, however, it is said that there is an Altar to the 8 Avatars of Reality. There is a legend that collecting all 8 stones and bringing them to the Altar will allow you to make a wish and travel to their realm.
