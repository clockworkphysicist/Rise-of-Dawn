# The Freelands

## History

Completely flat and with no rivers, geographical features, or even the most minor of change in it's plane; even the grass refuses to grow above ankle height. On some days, especially after rain, the clouds will seem to descend down onto the plain and it will appear as though one is walking on clouds.

However, between the countless bandit raiders and their camps, the high likelihood of getting lost, and the lack of food, water, or life for thousands of miles, it is best avoided.
