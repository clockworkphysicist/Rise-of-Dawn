# Dechestus

## Description

The Grand College of Dechestus is, for all intents and purposes, a nation state unto itself. Nestled deep in the heart of Katael, the Mages College is founded within the oldest and largest Exalic Citadel known to man, and within it's walls lie countless magical artefacts, scrolls, and tomes. Dechestus contains not only the some of the most esteemed classes in all of Aldiin, but facilities such as the Hall of Attunement, the Office of Replication, the Grand Archive of Azarlan, and the mysterious Chamber of Tasalen.

Though the city itself is able to be entered by all, only those born in the city of pure blood may join the ranks of the college under the supervision of it's Council of Professors. Those of deep, innate, magical ability or those children of the faculty are enrolled from birth and raised by the college itself.
