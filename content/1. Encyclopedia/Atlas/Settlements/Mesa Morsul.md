# Mesa Morsul

## Description

Located in Alpha Sector, Mesa Morsul is the brain of Cyberlink. A towering monolith to Science, Research, and Advancement, the Research City acts as, in effect, the "Capitol of Aldiin". The base floors are the showcase rooms, then above that is the amenities, above that is the medical section, above that is the security sector, and then above that is the staff housing, and at the very top is admin. However, due to the size and scale of the building, the way that the segments are set up is that all the way down the middle of the tower are various laboratories and research facilities. The facilities themselves are certified for experiments up to Hazard Level Daedalus. Being the capitol of Alpha Sector, it is the seat of Karamazov's top researchers, which administrate the entire city alongside AI assistance.

Each Sector has labs cleared for different Hazard Levels on an increasing scale: Ariadne, Theseus, Daedalus, and Icarus.
