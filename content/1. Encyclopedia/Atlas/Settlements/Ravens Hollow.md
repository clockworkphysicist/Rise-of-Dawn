# Ravens Hollow

## Description

A village near the Oreyenic Border in the Ascalonian province of Gothia, Ravens Hollow has a reputation for being a rather peaceful and storied, albeit somewhat mysterious town. It had its origins in a local folk tale involving a family descending from Ascalonian nobles who had, supposedly, partook in Vampiric ritual magic in order to rid themselves of the Ashen curse, unable to cope with their bloodline no longer holding its divinity from the dragon covenant. There, it is said, lies Castle Ravencrest; hidden by a forest of redbud trees and impassable mountains.

Though this has been long viewed as only a myth, and the opening of the Grand University of Lunavale has since made Ravens Hollow a quaint little hamlet visited by college students on break, local superstitions around the old redbud forest still swirl…
