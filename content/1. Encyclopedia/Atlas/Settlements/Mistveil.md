# Mistveil

## Description

In the human confederacy, deep in the kingdom of Alpeś, there is a town which is perpetually covered with fog and mist which defies even the most powerful of magics. Indeed, the town itself seems to be a place where magic has no hold or presence… at least, not that which can cut through the fog itself. The fog, meanwhile, seems to have enchanted qualities about itself; not only quieting sounds within it, but seemingly having the ability to confuse even the landscape itself. Paths will lead to places they are not meant to, with some seemingly getting lost for days only to find themselves a few feet away from where they started.

The natives, however, have a means by which they cut through this fog; a mysterious sort of black lantern with a bluish green flame. The light seems to be able to cut through the fog, and protects those who wield it from the strange fogs otherworldly effects. The color of the flame seems to change in its vibrancy and color as it is imbued with more of the fogs presence, becoming a cyan blue when in the presence of powerful manipulations manifested by the fog… including strange shadowy figures. Though these figures seem to be nothing more then shadows that disappear as they are approached, those who see them swear that they feel eyes upon them. Watching them.

The towns main export is lumber and langoustine.
