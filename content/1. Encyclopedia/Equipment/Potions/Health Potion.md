# Health Potion

The humble health potion. Deceptively simple to make, the main ingredients are water, honey, and dried Fey Ear leaves. The potion's effects, as the name implies, heals the body through regenerating damaged tissue, supplementing lost blood, and acting as a strong painkiller. It is able to do this by stimulating the body's somacytes, however it is capable of generating pseudosomacytes in biological organisms that lack them. For this reason, it is also the base of the rare but powerful "Cure Illness" potion.
