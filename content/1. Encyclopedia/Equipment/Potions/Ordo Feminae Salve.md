## Significance

When it comes to training, The Ordo Feminae tries to work in the framework of the desires of those who come to them as initiates, and the Mother Butterfly of the order helps create a "vision" for a person to help guide them towards embracing femininity to as much as an extreme that's considered possible. A tool that is commonly used in this process are various salves made using highly advanced alchemical processes. These salves can have varying effects, such as causing swelling or shrinking when applied to areas of the body, removal of facial and body hair, and even clearing the skin of marks or scars. Because of the salves rarity, potency, and with sustained use, permanency, their usage is highly restricted.
