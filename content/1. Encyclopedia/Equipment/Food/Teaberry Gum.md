## Description

## Significance

A humble, unassuming pack of gum. Teaberry is a favorite for many in Alinton who swear by it's use in helping with withdrawal from addiction. As a result, many recovering drug addicts, alcoholics, and wizards are known for chewing it, making the gum a sort of unspoken shibboleth among these groups.
