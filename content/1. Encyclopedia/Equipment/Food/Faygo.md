# Faygo

## Description

The favored drink of choice for the Juggalos of Ord Gulley in North Alinton, Faygo comes in many flavors and colors. Though just a simple and cheap soda brand, there is a commonly held belief that cutting it with different potions can enhance the effects. It is also, according to the inhabitants of the Posse, a good way to stabilize the effects of magical overcast.
