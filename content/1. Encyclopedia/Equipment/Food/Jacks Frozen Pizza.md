# Jacks Frozen Pizza

## Description

Referred to as just "a pizza" throughout the D.E.Z. Jacks Pizza is an anomalous "food" item which seems to be a staple among the lower classes across Aldiin. It is, in effect, a frozen pizza which is bought in bulk in supermarkets for a scant few dollars. Little is known about it's distribution, as the address which is labeled on the packaging leads to the middle of nowhere. It is a commonly held urban myth in Aldiin that the level to which you cook the pizza will imbue it with different effects, but this seems to be unsubstantiated. Sometimes purchased as cat food.
