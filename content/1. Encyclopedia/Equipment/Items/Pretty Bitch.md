# Pretty Bitch

## Description

N-(2,6-dimethylphenyl)-5,6-dihydro-4H-1,3- thiazin-2-mythrilamine, known primarily as "Pretty Bitch" or "Mythic Tranq", is a Schedule 2 sedative. Synthesized by causing a mithril reaction with Xylaxine, a horse tranquilizer, Pretty bitch is considered one of the most troublesome drugs to be present in the D.E.Z., it's use is most common in human trafficking and slavery rings. It received it's name, along with it's reputation, because it is a drug that masks any visible symptoms of withdrawal or addiction to the body outside of visible signs of secondary side effects. It becomes even more dangerous when mixed with fentanyl; a pharmaceutical drug whose illicit use was pioneered by the Unholy Qabalah. Mixing these two drugs, Alintons most notorious gang often administers small doses to their high flight risk prostitutes not only to keep them more docile when working, but establish a level of physical addiction.
