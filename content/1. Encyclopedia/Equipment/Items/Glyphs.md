# Glyphs

## Description

Imbued with a single spell, these thin, coin sized stones are used to attune those spells and grant them to a caster. When placed in an attunement table, the caster only needs to place their palms on indicated areas of the table after clicking the glyph into the slot and focus. Upon attuning, the user will feel an deep emotion resonating with the spell for a short moment, indicating that the spell is equipped. The glyph will then break, becoming just a stone once more. One with little experience in magic may only attune one spell at a time, whereas those more experienced may attune up to five, or even more depending on their level of skill.

Portable, disposable, and convenient; these containers of magic have been used for millennia, and they often fetch a pretty penny at any market. Only those who are truly learned in the enchanting arts may make them, such as those among the ranks of the magic crafters.
