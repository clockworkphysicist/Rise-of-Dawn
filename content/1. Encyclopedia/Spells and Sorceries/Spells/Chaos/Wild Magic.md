# Wild Magic

## Effects

Wild Magic Casting, by nature, has no consistency. No real rhyme or reason. It is simply following the chaotic, intrusive thoughts wherever they go and following the outcome for no reason whatsoever. Because of this, the effects of Wild Magic are just as varied as they are nonsensical. They can be exactly what you need, or precisely what you fear.

## Notes

As one gains more experience as a Chaos Caster, they will be able to narrow down further and further the amount of effects the Wild Magic Table has, and can learn abilities or gain items that will negate unwanted effects or allow for re-rolling. (1d100 -> 1d90 -> 1d80 ect. Minimum 1d10.)
