## History

Three days after Rylor was supposedly slain, The Black Temple, Dirus Angelus, rose from the freelands along with him. He had taken on a dark appearance, as well as the moniker of “The Man of Sin” from his followers, and all were eager to find the Dark Rock on which he would found his temple. He is a vessel and manifestation of the King of Demons, the master to whom Wormwood bows his head.

He has many titles, and though the church still simply refers to him as “Archbishop Rylor”, he is, in effect “The Deceiver”.
