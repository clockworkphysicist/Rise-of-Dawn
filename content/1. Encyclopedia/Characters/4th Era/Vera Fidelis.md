## Notes

She desired attention from authority figures. There are certain things she remembers of her mother, but not much. That said, the nun than ran the orphanage she was raised in was horrible; an abusive monster who frequently demanded perfection and would take out her anger on the children. Vera's consistent childhood memory was listening for the clack of her heels to hear when she was coming; usually being the one to have to care for the other kids after the storm had passed. Vera would always have to show her best to avoid getting punished, being scolded for being a little devil even for small accidents, worst of all when she would accidentally refer to the sister as "mommy" instead of "Mother Superior". It wasn't until much later when the orphanage got staffed with a second nun that Vera opened up and told her what she experienced that the other nun filed a report with the archbishop, though he was slow to act.

Vera has mommy issues that are so deeply repressed that she never even recognized they were there. Female authority figures are something Vera has a level of terror in regards to, but also a sort of Freudian sexual frustration towards, especially in terms of it being directed towards her image of herself. There's a lot of repressed paraphilia deep in her psyche due to childhood experiences with abandonment and abuse, outwardly manifesting in some ways as her developing inclinations towards being useful, guiding others, and people being dependent on her. Demons have the ability to instantly know all of this just by looking at someone. All of this was taken into consideration, and her body was warped as a sort of mockery that stabs directly at her heart.
