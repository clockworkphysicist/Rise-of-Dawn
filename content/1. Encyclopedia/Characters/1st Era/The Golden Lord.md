# The Golden Lord

## Appearance

The Golden Lord, true to his name, is adorned in armor of shimmering gold.

## Abilities

## History

Elias was a human from Rosanne who was, supposedly, given power by the gods that none other could rival; a weapon that could change to fit whatever his needs, the ability to construct entire cities from nothing, complete invulnerability, and above all the ability to turn anything he touched to pure gold. Building an army beyond compare, he conquered tribe after tribe and kingdom after kingdom as his people, the humans, began to regard him as a god in his own right. His armies marched on Aldiin with him at the helm, and in his might he created five cities of gold in the five realms of the land as an eternal reminder of his power and right to rule over all of the races and nations of Aldiin. However, no matter how powerful, he could not escape mortality. On his final march to the lands of Muerteria, his army shattered against the shores of the undead. In his last moments, overtaken with undeath, he proclaimed that his army would go to the strongest. Thus, he gave his second in command this order, and passed on. Word of his final words spread throughout the human lands, and eventually a noble found his way to the lands of Muerteria and found the Golden Lords corpse. After battling the shambling husk of the dead god with an army of his own, the noble slit Elias' wrist and, wishing to consume his power, drank his decayed blood. The noble gained dustings of the Golden Lords powers, and more importantly, the right to rule all of mankind; to the humans, all of Aldiin, in fact. This is known as the dispensation of the Golden Lord, where the throne of the Kingdom of Golddale rests it's power.
