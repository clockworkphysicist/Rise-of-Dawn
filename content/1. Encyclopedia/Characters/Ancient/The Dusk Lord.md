## History

The Dusk Lord was once the prince of the land of Illydar, named Indrell Carathor. Illydar had been torn by civil war for centuries. However, he received a prophecy from a Luminary of the Oyeresu, who told him that it was his destiny to stop the coming of a great apocalypse and save his people. To fulfill this destiny, he ventured out to unify and gain the power of four thrones established on Aldiin, the thrones of Illuen. By using them, he could tap into the Mystical Realm. Though once sheltered, as he progressed on his journey, he witnessed more and more horrors that began to haunt him, driving him deeper into desperation to protect his people. To this end, he amassed more and more power and magical enchantments, which he kept in a castle on the moon of Ulendar. His paranoia reached a fever pitch as the Illydaran people of the Authdis enclave were enslaved by the Tyrant King Valkesh, who had begun to ritually sacrifice them, calling on dark powers from beyond the material realm. Desperate to save them and becoming maddened by the weight of expectations on him, Indrell started to try and call on those same powers for the greater good. From deep within himself, he used his fears, anxieties, and the depths of his psyche to create all manner of Eldritch beasts, which would eventually be known as "The Ancients". With his people being slaughtered by Valkesh, he used his army of Ancients to attack Valkesh in a final assault on the corrupted land of Olteris, "The Land of the Dusk". Taking the power of Dusk for himself, he faced Valkesh at Mount Carrinth, beating him down. As Valkesh lay defeated, his emotions surged like a flurry of chaos as Indrell channeled all of the deep magic within himself and all of the powers he had obtained in order to strike Valkesh down with fury. The resulting strike was powerful enough to rip the veil between the physical and metaphysical realm asunder, blanketing the world in chaotic magic.

Illydar, situated at the convex of the world, sank into the ocean, but was kept in stasis by the remaining light within Indrell. Broken, Indrell had become the very apocalypse he was destined to prevent, yet his mind was fixated only on one thing: He needed more power. He needed to undo what had been done. He needed more magic. This maddening depth was so great that within the moon of Ulendar, a star began to form, one which devoured the very magic that had been produced by the chaotic maw of the inferno. Thus, the Lord of Dusk ravaged the land, absorbing all magic within himself as the Void Star burned brighter. Seeing the chaos that was ensuing, the Oyeresu sought to bring an end to the Dusk Lord, only to find that their powers were not nearly enough. As Leo, Oyarsa of Might, clashed with the Dusk Lord, he found that even his divine power was being consumed by the Eldritch Deep. Realizing the threat not only to the world but to themselves as well, Astaria, Oyarsa of Knowledge, locked the Dusk Lord away in his castle on the moon of Ulendar, trapped forever with his immense power, alone and with the memories of his people and the man he was eventually being consumed by the Void Star that was his very spirit.
