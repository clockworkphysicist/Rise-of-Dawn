## History

Kiera Maharn is the Arch-General of the Ascalonian Army. She was possessed by a demon, an incarnation of the Avatar of Hate, Agrah, as she was forming in her mothers womb. Agrah was a powerful demon who killed Kieras father, a gladiator, and ripped off his skin to wear. While masquerading as him, Agrah laid with his wife, eventually chaining her up and forcing her to give birth to a child that would encompass his mark so that it would be vessel for him so that he could begin an era of slaughter. This backfired, as the baby was female, and was deemed unfit to be controlled by the demon. Agrah threw it to the tigers to eat. However, the newborn infant ripped them apart with her bare hands. Recoiling in horror at the unrestrained power of the vessel he created, Agrah swiftly went to possess the baby, but found that her all consuming rage could not be bested, and as such was trapped in a constant battle of his own creation.

As she grew, so did her homicidal desires and might, but wanting to protect her mother, the only person in her life who loved her, from what she saw as an inevitable violent end, she joined the army in order to gain an endless outlet for her to carry out her desire for bloodshed. However, after seeing her first battle, she was overtaken by a feeling of unease as she witnessed the strange, paradoxical humanity of war. Over time, she bonded with two fellow cadets, Izrathel (Vincent) and his best friend Savicci; who slowly began to bring out more and more of her as a person beyond the mountain rending killing machine. However, as she developed romantic feelings for Savicci, he was killed on the battlefield leading to her becoming withdrawn and morose. Learning the value of life, she swore to protect her last remaining friend with everything in her power.
