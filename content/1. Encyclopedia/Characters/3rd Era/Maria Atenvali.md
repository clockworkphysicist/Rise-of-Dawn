## History

Maria, daughter of St. Mary, was a student at the College of Dechestus, but dropped out when Prof. Sarah was fired and the regime changed, and decided to found her own school. She found Moricia, who had revered Maria as the daughter of St. Mary, and the two got a friendship after learning to accept that being the daughter of a saint doesn't make you a saint. While this was an important lesson Moricia learned, Maria felt the crushing weight of the expectations of "holy blood" and became even more desperate to live up to her mothers connection to the divine. She felt ashamed, and as she and Moricia began to work on founding the school, she went on an adventure with Tarron and his friends to try and find the answer to her pain and awaken the full power of her vision, letting Moricia know that once her adventure was over and she cleared her mind, then she would decide if she was no longer a fuck up. During a battle, however, she had to use her powers to teleport a dragon into space, and it landed her on the moon. Suffocating, and seeing visions of her mother, she uses her necklace to freeze herself in crystal, believing she can control it to then return her back home (as the crystal will transport to the closest safe place when she unfreezes). However, she once again fucks up and freezes herself for almost 800 years. She unfreezes and returns home, running back to Lunavale in hopes of seeing Moricia and the small school, only to be met with a giant university she co-founded and a friend who had long passed. In despair, she is about to give up on magic and ever trying to awaken her powers again, only to have a dream with Moricia, her mother, her father, and Adger. They tell her to not give up on herself, and she applies to the school as a student, no one believing her claim that she was one of the founders. She takes it to heart and says that, no, she may as well have not been its founder, but she WILL be the woman to guide the school as it was meant to be.
