## History

The crest of the Everglade Family was specifically designed by Queen Amira upon her ascension to the throne, a duty neglected by her late husband. Featuring the Golden Lion of Golddale in the center, it also included four vines at the four corners of the crest; Parsley, Sage, Rosemary and Thyme. The four vines were chosen based on an ancient, many considered lost, traditional song of Middleton; preserved only in the deep cultural consciousness of the peasantry.

Though her reign was fraught with controversy, the usage of the four vines on the crest of humanity was beloved both within and, to the surprise of many, outside of Middleton itself. It was, however, it’s usage and association with the golden age of humanity under Queen Nicadea that it became a symbol of humankind, as well as a rallying cry of sorts; to the point that even after the Everglade family came to an end, future generations of human nobles would use them in their heraldry.

Ironic, in a sense, that though Queen Amira feared her child would be the end of her family line, they were in truth the key that led to it being immortalized in human culture for millennia after.
