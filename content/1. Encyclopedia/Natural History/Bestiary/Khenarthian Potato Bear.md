# Khenarthian Potato Bear

## Description

The Kenarthian Potato Bear, known in it's native language as Niedźwiedź ziemniaczany, is in actuality not truly an individual species, but rather a brown bear that has been infected with Potato Blight. These bears are more often than not found in the Głuchy Woods, and are the result of an infestation of a vegetable borne parasite which uniquely affects the Brown Bear species. It will often give off an aroma that is undetectable by humans, but irresistible to the brown bear. The infected potato will then transfer a parasite which has the unique property of tuberfication, in which the insides of the bear are turned into a starch while it is still alive. Over time, though the process is painless, the bear will begin to behave erratically, and often more violently as though it were intoxicated; it's body being overtaken with buds which blossom within the bears muscles. The roots will strengthen the bears muscles, and create a defensive carapace as it's innermost layer of skin becomes similar to that of the skin of a potato. At it's furthest extent, the infection causes the main tuber within the bear to gain an ocular port on the side, which allows the now hijacked bear to be directed by the parasitic potato. The potatoes control over the bear will be bolstered by three large russet potatoes which sprout from the buds on the bears back. The end of this process results in a Potato Bear.

Though it is extremely dangerous, it will sometimes be hunted by multiple hunters for food, given that not only can it's meat and infected body be used to make a meat and potato stew, but that it's bladder, liver and stomach actually produce an unrefined vodka.

It's brain actually is no longer functioning by the time it is a full on potato bear (at least, not except for movement and reactions, but even those can be directly puppeted by the potato if it needs to be). The bear itself is controlled by the potato within it, however it's biggest weakness is actually the potato nodes that grow from it. See, the bear is infected with tendril like vines that can extend out from it and strangle/constrict prey, however if the potato nodes are targeted then the potato loses control of the bear, and the bear slumps over: the potato will then open it's ocular port to examine it's surroundings first hand, and that's when it's at it's weakest.
