# Ayabex Ram

## Description

An agressive, hearty animal known for its propensity to ram unsuspecting travelers or animals off of cliff sides as a show of dominance. It’s multi-layered, twirelled horns which face forward are often tough enough to glance blows from steel, and it’s neck muscles, as well as the density of it’s skull, make it quite the powerhouse. Hunting such animals is ill advised, as they tend to stay near precarious positions on cliffsides, hunkering down low to the ground and close to walls in order to better defend itself. Though mainly considered a pesky nuisance by Elves, the other races, lacking in the Elves regenerative abilities, may find these animals to be an extreme hazard to be avoided at any cost when traveling.
