# Lesser Demon

## Description

Lesser demons are, essentially, horrific monsters which appear from the infernal realm, fueled by the void of "self" and formed by the chaos of emotion and irrationality within the realm of magic that is created as a result of the privation of divinity. They are demons in the sense that they are creations of the infernal, but don't seem to "come from" the same place as greater demons.
