# Zombie

## Description

Zombies are an unfortunate but terrifying situation in which a person dies too far away from their natural lifespan, died with regrets or final wishes, had a deep seated fear of dying, and felt emotions so strongly that it basically enchanted their corpse post Mortem to trap their spirit inside of it and chain their soul into that spirit. Though zombies mumble and murmur, their minds are long gone and the enchantment can’t keep their bodies from rotting. They shamble towards people to claw and bludgeon; as though they’re sluggishly, but desperately trying to find someone else to save them despite being beyond help. Perhaps they are, paradoxically, trying to find someone to destroy them. That’s where the Muerterians come in. Rather than destroy them, they use their magic to sort their body, soul, and spirit out and give them their remaining time as a conscious being to reflect, meditate, and eventually find peace in death.
