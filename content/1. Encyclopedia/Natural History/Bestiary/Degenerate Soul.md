# Degenerate Soul

## Description

Sometimes, when one is between the physical realm of Aldiin and the metaphysical realm of hell, they manifest as a degenerate soul. A Degenerate Soul is a being whose horrific and twisted spirit ties their tainted, damned soul to the mortal realm, twisting their bodies into horrific forms. Degenerate Souls are neither living nor dead; while they are surely not dead, and can be killed, to call them alive is an insult to life itself. They are essentially people who have hedged themselves between the stratum of lesser and greater demon just by their sheer evil.
