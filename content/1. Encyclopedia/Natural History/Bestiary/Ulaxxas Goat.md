# Ulaxxas Goat

## Description

With it’s long legs and back facing horns, the Ulaxxas Goat is well known for both it’s high milk output and it’s heavy, thick wool. Able to survive on the low oxygen, high altitude areas of the mountains, this beast mostly eats grass, siltweed, and fungi. These animals are prized throughout the Elven world, as both farmers in the low grassy valleys and shepherds on the high trails have these beasts as their main livestock. As the high mountainous kingdoms tend to have little in the way of arable land, these animals are seen as the primary source of food.
