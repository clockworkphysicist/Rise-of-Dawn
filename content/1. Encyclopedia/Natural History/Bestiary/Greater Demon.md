# Greater Demon

## Description

These beings are the metaphysical reality of the infernal made manifest: essentially physical apparitions of true demons which anchor themselves to the physical plane. They are beings that are, essentially, completely devoid of divinity, and thus appear almost more in feeling than form; they cause horror, anxiety, and a feeling of just generally things not being "right" just by their presence. Unlike lesser demons, who seem to manifest _from_ the infernal in Aldiin, greater demons just don't seem... natural.
