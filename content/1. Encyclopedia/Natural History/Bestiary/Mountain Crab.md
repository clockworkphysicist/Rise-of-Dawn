# Mountain Crab

## Description

Long, imposing, and eerie, these lanky creatures can be found clinging tightly to the sides of mountains. With a body roughly the size of a shield and legs around five meters in length, these light grey crustaceans appear as giant spider crabs, blending in to mountain sides. They are slow creatures, sometimes allowing moss and other plant life to grow on it as it slowly moves about and soaks in moisture from the clouds. The Great King Mountain Crab is a gigantic version of this animal, and it has been said that they can span the face of a small mountain.
