# Magic Crafting

## Description

Magic crafting requires specific items and actions, as well as catalyzing ones own emotions to focus on a general effect. These items can be almost anything, and the actions needed to gain insight on how to use them in the crafting process and obtaining the spell can range from "Touch your nose and wiggle your feet" to "Sacrifice 1000 newborn infants", and you just kind of have to feel what the action needed to gain the spell is.

A crafted spell cannot be anything that is too specific, and must use a combination of already possible effects.
