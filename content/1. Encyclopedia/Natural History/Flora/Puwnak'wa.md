# Puwnak'wa

## Description

The Puwnak'wa, or "Imbvari Cheese Bush" is a unique type of bush whose buds, left over time, are broken down by enzymes to produce a fatty substance inside of bulbs sprouting from the vines. This substance, over time and in the sun, is mixed with sugars and proteins from the plant to produce a plant based cheese.

The natives of Keshak’iosa province often collect these bulbs and use the cheese in dishes. It is described as being akin to mozzarella, with a hint of cheddar to it's smell and taste.
