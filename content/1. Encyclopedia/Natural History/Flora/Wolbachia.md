# Wolbachia

## Description

Wolbachia is a strain of bacteria that, when introduced into someone in great amounts who is affected by the Bacchus Virus, will turn them into a drow; turning their skin purple, their hair lavender, changing aspects of their body, and making them easy to mind control or be used for breeding as, for women, their bodies are modified to have many offspring at once.
