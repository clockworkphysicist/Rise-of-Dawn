# Kiri no Sakura

## Description

This tree is known for it's unique pink petals and pale white bark; specifically the pedals unique properties of turning into a fine powder upon contact with hard surfaces. Traditionally, this pink powder has many different applications, such as for dye and cosmetics, but most surprisingly, as a culinary ingredient. The powder has a very potent sweet flavor, when mixed with gum from the bark of the tree, it is used to create a sort of candy. The pedals are also traditionally used as a sweetening agent in tea.
