# Kulla Virus

## Description

The Kulla Virus is an offshoot of the Dryad Queens Rot, a Malefic Malady. It manifests as black mold which causes decay in living organisms. It's presence can be detected, apart from the signs of delirium and weakness, through the blood being turned black and somewhat 'chunky'. It is one of the few ways living zombification can occur; the others being chaos magic, psionic bursts, and tryacic radiation.
