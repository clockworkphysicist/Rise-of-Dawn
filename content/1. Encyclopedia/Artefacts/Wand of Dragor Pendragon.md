# Wand of Dragor Pendragon

## Description

Dragor's resentment toward his lineage and irreverence for the seemingly arbitrary boundaries mankind had placed on itself in the pursuit of learning drove him further and further away from his family, his master, and his faith. Even during his time training under Lady Maria at the University of Lunavale, his wand was one designed with the depths of his spirit and the goal of his learning in mind—a signature look that concerned his master. The supreme tool for Eldritch Sorceries.
