# Wand of Lady Maria

## Description

The wand of Lady Maria the Astral Sorcerer is one of the most treasured relics in the world of magic. Created while studying under the inventor of the wand, Sir Adger Tharne, Maria was instructed in every facet of it's form. This led to her creating a wand which used a non-standard base of lapis, a traditional eight-gate and gold circuit configuration, and most impressively, a crystal foci made from crystals from the protective barrier her father preserved her life in; the obtainment sphere replaced with a crystal star that spins on a armillary ring.

This wand was, due to it's personalized style and magical form, one of the most effective and powerful wands ever created. It was because of this very wand that the art of wandmaking became as prominent as it did. Maria, since her work under master Tharne, always had a connoisseur like fascination with them.
