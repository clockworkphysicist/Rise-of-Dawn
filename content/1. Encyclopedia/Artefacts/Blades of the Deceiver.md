## Notes

The Sabbath lamb had been slaughtered. It was finished. When Rylor awoke, he was not himself. He was something greater. Begotten, not made. Consubstantial with...

He was not recognized by his followers, at first, but he made himself known to them. Just as he had bequeathed the keys to his kingdom to his faithful disciple, his disciple gave him the fruit of his labors: his regalia. An ornate robe which would be proclaimed as the cloak of the righteous, and the swords which would act as his scepter and sovereigns orb. Accursed silver blades that bared his visage.
