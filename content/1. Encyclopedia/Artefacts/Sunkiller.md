# Sunkiller

## Description

Sunkiller is a cursed blade, created with an unspeakable amount of anger, spite, and sorrow. When unsheathed, Sunkiller has the harrowing ability to snuff out all nearby light sources, dimming the world around it. It's second, and more dangerous enchantment, is the unique fire that burns within it; the Soulflame. An extension of the it's wielders soul, Sunkiller burns with a sickly green flame which seems to be of dark origin. Those who touch the Soulflame find their magical ability weakened, and a sense of guilt and dread burned into their soul as the flames burn the very spirit. However, at some point Sunkillers flames appear to have manifested a unique attribute from the carving scrawled into it's blade, where it's flame cannot burn anyone who is not in a state of 'grave sin'. As well, the flames appear to have a blessed attribute to them, which makes them especially effective against demonic entities. It cannot leave the presence of it's wielder, and will always be nearby; bound to them for the rest of their life.
