# The Atlas

## Description

Atlas is an book which is completely empty, and seems to have infinite pages which cannot be written in and cannot be removed. Whoever takes ownership of it will have everything they learn about and gain knowledge of recorded in the Atlas, and the more knowledge they gain on something the more details will be filled in. With the wave of ones hand, the pages will turn to the article one desires to know, however it can also be read front to back like a book. When the person who has ownership of it dies, or elsewise loses their status as a mortal being, everything in the Atlas will be erased, starting from square one for the next person who picks it up.
