# Stratovarius

## Description

Stratovarius, or as it is known, "The Blade of Infinity", is a legendary sword which is said to be enshrined at the very center of the Azgora grasslands. Forged through mystical sorceries, it is designed to hold the eight avatar stones of destiny, of which it can draw power from to coalesce into an 'Eternal Light'. The blade is trailed by a rainbow when swung.
