# Na'wenya

## Description

Considered to be the beating heart of the center of Imbvara, the land of the Gnomes, it takes on the name "Na'wenya" or "Elthali" to the Imps. An item of great ritual and cultural significance, it is a flat golden disk which represents the Sun and it's movements across the sky in alignment with the constellations, and thus the Solar Spirit. The Matriarch of Imbvara, once per year at the time of Autumnal Equinox, places the Na'wenya in a mausoleum so that it may rest, alongside the Impic Princess who betrothed it.
