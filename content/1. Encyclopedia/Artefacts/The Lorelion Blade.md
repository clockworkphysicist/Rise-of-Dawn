# The Lorelion Blade

## Description

A mysterious sword found in the abandoned Sanctum of Lorelia, this swords history is shrouded in myth. Said to have been caried into battle once by a prince of Agadain, the blade is of remarkable craftsmanship. However, the blades remarkable feature is that it appears to adapt and evolve with it's weilder; gaining enchantments and changing it's design the more it's owner gains experience. This results in the blade becoming an extension of it's wielder, responding to none but the one who it is connected to. Because of these factors, the blade is assumed to have a strong mystical component to it's construction, though how it was achieved and what lay behind it's creation is a tale likely lost to time.
